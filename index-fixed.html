// METHODOLOGY · FABRICATION

Four Layers, One Board

EcoShield's fabrication builds outward from a natural fibre blend to a fully networked sensing surface — each layer adding a distinct capability without adding a distinct material cost.

01

Fibre Core

Coir and casuarina fibre blended and hot-pressed into the structural base board, tuned for a balance of tensile strength and low density.
HYBRID BLEND · 3.2–4.5mm CORE
02

Fire Treatment

Intumescent surface coating applied to the pressed core, delaying ignition and slowing flame spread across the panel face.
SURFACE COAT · INTUMESCENT
03

Sensor Layer

Flame sensor, MPU6050 vibration/motion sensor, and a heat-rise temperature probe embedded into a routed channel beneath the treated face.
FLAME · MPU6050 · TEMP PROBE
04

Connectivity

ESP32-WROOM-32 microcontroller reads all three sensors over I2C/analog and reports status to the site network for real-time monitoring.
ESP32-WROOM-32 · WI-FI / BLE
