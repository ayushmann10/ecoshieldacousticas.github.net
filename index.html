Methodology · Fabrication

Four Layers, One Board

From natural fibre blend to a networked sensing surface.

01 · FIBRE CORE

Coir and casuarina fibre

Coir and casuarina fibre blended and hot-pressed into the structural base board, tuned for a balance of tensile strength and low density.

HYBRID BLEND · 3.2–4.5mm CORE

02 · FIRE TREATMENT

Intumescent surface coating

A surface coating is described as delaying ignition and slowing flame spread across the panel face.

SURFACE COAT · INTUMESCENT

03 · SENSOR LAYER

Embedded sensor module

Flame sensor, MPU6050 vibration/motion sensor, and heat-rise temperature probe are embedded into a routed channel beneath the treated face.

FLAME · MPU6050 · TEMP PROBE

04 · CONNECTIVITY

ESP32-WROOM-32

The ESP32 reads the sensors over I2C/analog and reports status for real-time monitoring.

WI-FI / BLE
